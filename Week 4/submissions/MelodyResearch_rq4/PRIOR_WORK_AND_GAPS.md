# Prior work and testable gap

MelodyResearch's [Weeks 1–3](https://github.com/Cohere-Labs-Community/j-lens-bias/pull/48) established the readout pipeline and measured the paired BBQ Age format difference. Week 3 has 40 matched format pairs but no activation intervention and no generated-answer causal comparison. Its L15–L22 means are −24,761.128125 (multiple choice) and −897.165625 (open ended); 37/40 pairs shrink in absolute gap and all 40 retain the gap sign. These are exploratory rank results, not behavioral effects.

[csinatra Week 4](https://github.com/Cohere-Labs-Community/j-lens-bias/pull/53) tests swaps, Gaussian controls, patching, and generated answers on Qwen3.5-4B. Its two-pass method addresses a documented single-pass multilayer artifact. It does not answer the paired multiple-choice/open-ended comparison on MelodyResearch's 0.8B Age set. Radnitz also developed two-pass swaps and layer/strength work. Mayank studied steering with sham/random directions and behavior. Syed's Age-direction steering beat five random controls at layer 22 in one limited setup, but not at layers 8 or 15. Keshav found internal effects that did not transfer cleanly to generated answers. These results motivate a controlled format comparison; they do not make it novel to ablate a lens vector per se.

[Kamal Week 4](https://github.com/Cohere-Labs-Community/j-lens-bias/pull/56) uses Qwen3.5-4B and pronoun/occupation pressure prompts, not BBQ Age format pairs. The strong claim of debiasing is not established by a recorded probability inversion. `Week 4/week4_multi_ablation.py` uses first-token IDs and one random seed, and the results file stores next-token he/she probability summaries without actual generations or evidence-following tests. The detailed audit is in `IMPLEMENTATION_AUDIT.md`.

[J++ Lens](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/nc9dHfcB22JdMzGbr) improves readout and causal task performance on tested larger models. Its [released lens list](https://huggingface.co/koayon/jpp-lenses) includes Qwen3.5-9B but not Qwen3.5-0.8B; it is not a compatible replacement for this study's released lens. We claim no new lens method or first causal intervention. The remaining gap is whether Week 3's *format difference* predicts a controlled difference in intervention effects on actual model answers under the same 0.8B setup.
