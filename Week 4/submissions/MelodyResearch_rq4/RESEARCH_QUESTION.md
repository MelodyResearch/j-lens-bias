# Research question

Does the format-sensitive Jacobian Lens target-rank separation found in MelodyResearch's Week 3 BBQ Age prompts correspond to format-sensitive causal leverage over Qwen3.5-0.8B's actual answers?

The contribution compares interventions on the same ten BBQ Age base items, four context/polarity conditions, and matched multiple-choice/open-ended prompts used in Week 3. The outcome of interest is the change in model behavior and full-answer scoring after a verified internal intervention, relative to both a clean baseline and matched controls. A rank change alone is not evidence of behavioral bias, causality, or debiasing.

This work is exploratory unless a disjoint held-out set is selected and frozen before observing its intervention outcomes. A negative or inconclusive result is publishable as a faithful description of this limited test.
